## 1. Overview

**Mission:** "Transform lives through the magic of football" by removing financial, social, and cultural barriers to access. The foundation's stated principle is that "no player will be turned away."
*(Source: https://bloomsburyfootball.com/our-story, accessed 2026-05-05)*

**Beneficiaries:** Young people aged 3–18 across London, with specific inclusion programmes for low-income households (75% of participants receive subsidised places at 25–100% discount), refugees and asylum seekers, blind and partially sighted players, and young people with disabilities.
*(Source: https://bloomsburyfootball.com/, https://bloomsburyfootball.com/financial-support, accessed 2026-05-05)*

**Geographic area:** Nine London boroughs. Two confirmed venue areas are Camden (HQ at LABS Camden, The Stables Market, Chalk Farm Road, NW1 8AH) and White City. Specific nine boroughs not listed by name on public pages.
*(Source: https://bloomsburyfootball.com/our-story, https://bloomsburyfootball.com/sitemap.xml, accessed 2026-05-05)*

**Founded:** 2018. Founder Charlie Hyman began with a single session in Camden Town with four players.
*(Source: https://bloomsburyfootball.com/our-story, accessed 2026-05-05)*

**Current scale:** 6,500+ young people served weekly (as of early 2026).
*(Source: https://bloomsburyfootball.com/our-impact, accessed 2026-05-05)*

## 5. Strategic Ambitions

1. **20,000 young people playing weekly by 2028** — confirmed target (currently 6,500+).
2. Expand from 6 to **15 community hubs** (defined as 2km localised areas) by 2028.
3. Achieve **50:50 gender balance** across all programmes.
4. Focus new hubs on areas in the **bottom 5% of national deprivation rankings**.
5. Deepen impact measurement: longitudinal surveys twice yearly, Theory of Change framework developed with University of Westminster.

*(Source: https://bloomsburyfootball.com/our-impact, https://bloomsburyfootball.com/our-story, accessed 2026-05-05)*

## 6. Donor Profile Implications

The existing funder and supporter mix points toward a distinct HNW donor profile:

- **Finance / City connections:** Goldman Sachs, Evercore, CVC, Rothschild & Co, BGC, LocalGlobe (VC), and individual donor Stuart Roden (Lansdowne Partners) signal that the foundation already has warm relationships with senior finance professionals. The likely HNW prospect pool includes current and former partners at these firms.
- **Sport business networks:** Ed Woodward (Man Utd), Gabby Logan (BBC Sport), LALIGA, FIFA Foundation, and Wembley Stadium Foundation indicate strong sport-industry ties. Prospects likely include sports executives, club owners, and sports media figures.
- **Legal and professional services:** Mishcon de Reya on the corporate list suggests connections into London's legal elite — a segment known for significant charitable giving.
- **Philanthropy infrastructure:** The Julia Rausing Trust and Parasol Foundation are family philanthropy vehicles; Oak Foundation and Garfield Weston are major institutional funders. Cultivating their networks may yield peer introductions to other family offices.
- **Gary Lubner / This Day Foundation** is a particularly high-signal connection: Lubner (formerly Belron/Carglass) is a committed philanthropist. His formal appointment as President suggests a major-gift-level relationship and potential to open introductions to his peer network.
- **Gaps:** No named individual donors from tech, property, or media sectors are visible in public data — these represent prospecting gaps given the Foundation's London base and youth/social-impact positioning.
