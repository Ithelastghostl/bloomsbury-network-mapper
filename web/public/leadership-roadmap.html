◈ Executive Summary

  • The Network Mapper has ingested and analysed thousands of Charity Commission filings to build a structured intelligence layer of people, organisations, and their interconnections relevant to BFF fundraising.
  • Over 1,000 person entities have been mapped with wealth profiles, board memberships, and philanthropic histories — creating a searchable database of potential HNW supporters and their networks.
  • The platform already surfaces warm introduction routes through shared connections, enabling the fundraising team to approach prospects through trusted intermediaries rather than cold outreach.
  • This roadmap outlines 15 targeted improvements — from quick-win automations to strategic intelligence capabilities — that will transform the platform from a research tool into an active fundraising engine with measurable ROI.

▲ Investment & Returns

What focused development effort returns in fundraising efficiency

⏱
Time Saved Per Engagement
Automated dossiers and enrichment eliminate hours of manual research before each meeting. The fundraising team spends time building relationships, not searching Companies House.
▲
Higher Conversion Rates
Introduction route scoring and affinity matching ensure prospects are approached through the warmest possible channel with messaging aligned to their interests. Warmer intros convert at multiples of cold outreach.
◉
Better-Targeted Asks
Giving pattern analysis and wealth profiling inform appropriate ask amounts and timing. No more guesswork on whether to request five or six figures — the data guides the conversation.
▬
Reduced Wastage from Cold Approaches
Every hour spent on an unqualified lead is an hour not spent on a warm prospect. The pipeline dashboard and qualification scoring ensure effort flows to the highest-probability opportunities.
