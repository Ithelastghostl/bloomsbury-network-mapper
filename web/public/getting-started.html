How it works Getting started

Start here

What is this tool?

The Network Mapper helps us find and reach potential major donors. It holds a big list of wealthy individuals and the people and organisations connected to them, and it shows you how everyone is linked, so we can find a warm introduction instead of a cold call.

Think of it in three layers: the people we already have, the promising people we’ve discovered around them, and a map of how they all connect.

You can’t break anything by clicking around. The tool only shows information. It doesn’t change or send anything. The single action button is clearly labelled, and you choose when to use it.

Lists

Seeds

The people we already have. These are our existing supporters and target donors: the names from our own lists, the ones we’ve done proper research on.

At the top you’ll see a summary box with the combined estimated wealth of this group, how it splits across wealth levels, and who the best-connected people are. Below that is a table. Click any name to open their full page.

Use it to review the core relationships we already own.

Leads

Promising new people we’ve discovered. These aren’t on our original list. We found them through our seeds — people who sit on the same boards, fellow trustees, family members — and we already have some wealth information on them.

Use it to spot warm prospects worth pursuing. Open a Lead’s page and use the Augment & Persist button to ask for deeper research (explained below).

Pipeline

The backlog. People we’ve discovered but haven’t researched yet — we don’t know their wealth or background. This is the raw pool that becomes Leads once someone looks into them.

Use it to see what’s waiting and decide who to research next.

All

Everyone, in one place. The complete list of every person in the system — seeds, leads and pipeline together. Use the search and filters at the top to find anyone.

Reading a list

What the columns mean

Every list is a table with the same columns:

ColumnWhat it tells you
NameThe person. Click to open their full page.
WealthTheir wealth level as a coloured badge — gold means the wealthiest (£100M+), down to grey for unknown.
ScoreHow confident we are, as a small bar.
LinksHow many other people they’re connected to.
EvidenceHow many supporting facts we’ve gathered.
StateWhether they’ve been researched yet.

You can search by name, filter by wealth level, and sort by clicking a column heading. The wealthiest are shown first by default.

The map

Orbit

A picture of how people connect. Each dot is a person, and a line between two dots means they’re linked: they share a company, a board, a family tie, or a charity. Tightly connected groups cluster together and share a colour, so you can see the natural “circles” in the network at a glance.

  • Bigger dots = better-connected people.
  • Thicker lines = stronger links (connected in more than one way).
  • Click a dot to zoom into just that person’s circle. Click “Show all” to go back.
  • ⌘/Ctrl-click a dot to open that person’s full page.
  • Use the “+ Institutions” toggle (top right) to also show the companies that connect people.

Use it to find the warmest path between someone we know and someone we want to reach.

Institutions

People and the companies they run. Shows individuals linked to the businesses they direct or advise. Useful for understanding someone’s commercial world and who shares their boardrooms.

Charities

People and the good causes they back. Charities and foundations sit at the centre, surrounded by their trustees and supporters. A great place to spot shared philanthropic interests we can connect with.

Going deeper

A person’s page

Click any name to open their profile. It gathers everything we know in four cards:

Wealth Profile

Their wealth level, estimated net worth, where the money came from, and a summary.

Network Connections

Who they’re linked to and how — each name clickable to hop across the network.

Evidence

The facts behind the profile, each with its source link.

First Contact Intelligence

Talking points for an introduction: their background, directorships, and charity involvement.

The one action

Augment & Persist + the Augment Queue

On a person’s page you’ll see an Augment & Persist button. This is how you ask for deeper research on someone who looks promising.

  1. You click the button. The person is added to a research list. The button changes to “Queued for research”.
  2. They appear in the Augment Queue. That page lists everyone waiting for research.
  3. Research happens. Their wealth and background are looked up.
  4. Their profile fills in. Once done, their page shows the new wealth details and they move up into our researched contacts.
Nothing happens until you click. The tool never researches people on its own — you’re always in control of who gets looked into.

In a nutshell

The 30-second version

  • Seeds = people we already have. Leads = promising people we found. Pipeline = not researched yet.
  • Orbit shows how everyone connects — use it to find a warm introduction.
  • Click a name to see everything about a person.
  • Click Augment & Persist to request research on someone.
  • You can’t break anything — explore freely.
