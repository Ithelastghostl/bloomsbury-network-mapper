How it works Getting started

What is this tool?

The Network Mapper helps the fundraising team find and reach potential major donors. It holds two source lists — our 283 supporters and donors (the people we already know) and 68 HNW targets (the wealthy individuals we want to reach) — and maps the network between them.

The tool answers three questions: who are the high-net-worth individuals around us, how are they connected to people we know, and what is the warmest route to reach them.

The platform is organised around four phases, borrowed from military decision-making: Observe, Orient, Decide, Act.

You can't break anything by clicking around. The tool displays information. The only actions are sending a lead to the backlog and updating its status — both require an explicit click.

The four phases

Sections in the sidebar

  1. Observe — see the raw data and enriched profiles
  2. Orient — explore relationships and introduction paths
  3. Decide — ranked leads and scored introduction routes
  4. Act — the outreach backlog

Observe

See the data

This section is about looking at what we have. Raw source files and enriched profiles side by side.

Supporters Sheet / HNW Sheet

The original spreadsheets rendered as searchable, filterable tables. Every row shows the person's name, tier (Priority or Important), funder sub-type (Current donor, Strategic contact, Key introducer, Target donor), affiliation, and who introduced them. Use these to see exactly what came in from the source files.

Supporters (enriched)

Our 283 supporters and donors, enriched. These are the starting point for all introduction paths. Their profiles carry wealth estimates, evidence, connections, and the funder labels from the original spreadsheet (shown as gold badges on their profile).

HNW Targets (enriched)

The 68 high-net-worth individuals we want to reach. These are the destination. The platform maps paths from our supporters to these people through the network.

Leads

People we discovered through our network — co-directors, fellow trustees, family — who already have some wealth or evidence data. These are warm prospects.

All Entities

Every person in the system, unfiltered. Use search and filters to find anyone.

Pipeline

Discovered people not yet researched — the raw backlog waiting for characterisation.

Orient

Understand the relationships

Graphs and tables that show how people connect and where the paths are.

Orbit

A force-directed graph of how people cluster. Each dot is a person; lines mean they share a board, company, charity, or family tie. Colours indicate clusters. Supporters are teal, HNW targets are orange. Toggle "+ Institutions" to see the companies that connect people. Use the 1-hop only checkbox to hide second-order connections and see only direct contacts of supporters.

  • Bigger dots = more connections
  • Thicker lines = stronger bonds (multiple shared roles)
  • Click a dot to open a profile panel, double-click to isolate their neighbourhood
  • Cmd/Ctrl-click to open the full profile page

Supporter Reach

Which supporters have the biggest HNW network? Two modes:

  • HNW Contacts — supporter bubbles sized by how many HNW individuals they know directly (1st degree). Lines connect supporters to their HNW contacts.
  • Network Worth — bubbles sized by the total estimated net worth of everyone in their immediate network.

Toggle between Graph and Table view. The table is sortable by HNW count, network worth, reach, and tier.

Introduction Graph

Starting from our supporters, this graph walks outward two hops to show who we can reach. Colours tell you the distance:

  • Gold = supporter (we know them)
  • Teal = one hop away (a supporter knows them directly)
  • Grey = two hops away (reached via a one-hop person)
  • Orange = HNW target (the destination)

Use 1-hop only to strip out second-order connections. Switch between "People chain" and "Via organisations" to see the linking boards.

Introduction Routes

A searchable, filterable table of every person reachable from our supporters, with up to 3 scored introduction paths each. Click a row to expand and see all paths with their score breakdowns. Filter by category (HNW Target, Wealth Identified, Charity Donor).

Institutions / Charities

People linked to companies (Institutions) or charities and foundations (Charities). Useful for understanding someone's commercial or philanthropic world.

Decide

Prioritise who to pursue

Lead Generator

The centrepiece. Every non-supporter person is scored across four dimensions and ranked in a single table:

DimensionWeightWhat it measures
Connectivity20%How many network connections they have (more connections = more ways in)
Wealth30%Their estimated net worth, log-scaled (so a billionaire scores higher but a multi-millionaire still registers)
Paths30%Number and quality of introduction routes from our supporters (3 good paths beats 1)
Affinity20%Charity overlap, donor category, and whether they work in philanthropy (likely to be warm to our cause)

Click any column header to re-rank by that single dimension. Use the category filter to see only HNW Targets, Wealth Identified, Charity Donors, or Discovered. Each row has a score bar for every dimension and a "Send" button that pushes the lead to the Action Backlog.

The sub-pages (By Connectivity, By Network Worth, By Intro Paths, By Donor Affinity) show the same table pre-sorted by one dimension.

Act

Manage outreach

Action Backlog

Leads sent from the Lead Generator land here as tickets. Each ticket carries:

  • The person's name, bio, sector, and wealth band
  • The best introduction path and which supporter to ask
  • The lead score and category
  • Whether the person has been validated by a human

Click a ticket to expand it. You can change its status to track progress:

StatusMeaning
NewJust arrived from the Lead Generator
OutreachReady to approach the supporter for an introduction
ContactedIntroduction has been made or contact established
Information neededMore research required before moving forward
DeferredParked for now
WonSuccessful outcome
LostNot progressing

Filter by status using the chips at the top. Tickets marked "unvalidated" have not been confirmed by a human.

Other features

Global search (Cmd+K)

Press Cmd+K (or Ctrl+K) anywhere in the CRM to search all people and organisations. Results show seed info, introduction context, and category. Click a result to open their profile.

Person profile

Click any name to open their profile. It shows:

Seed info badges

Gold badges for funder sub-type (Strategic contact, Key introducer, etc.), tier, and source (Supporter / HNW Target).

Introduction paths

Up to 3 scored routes from our supporters, each with rank, score, chain, linking organisation, and a plain-English reason why this path is good.

Wealth profile

Estimated net worth, wealth band, source, and confidence.

Connections & evidence

Network links and the facts behind the profile, each with its source.

Augment & Persist

On a person's profile, this button queues them for deeper wealth research by a local agent. The person appears in the Augment Queue, gets researched, and their profile fills in.

In a nutshell

  • Observe — see our source spreadsheets and enriched data side by side.
  • Orient — explore the network graph, supporter reach, and introduction paths. Use 1-hop toggle to simplify.
  • Decide — the Lead Generator ranks everyone by connectivity, wealth, paths, and affinity. Click columns to re-rank.
  • Act — send leads to the backlog, track outreach status from New through to Won.
  • Cmd+K to search anywhere. Click any name for a full profile.
